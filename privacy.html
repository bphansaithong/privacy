B. Phansaithong Holdings Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how B. Phansaithong Holdings (“we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information in connection with our websites, video games, mobile applications, and related online services (collectively, the “Services”). The exact information handled may vary by game, platform, and features you use.

1. Information We May Collect

Depending on the Services, we may collect information you provide directly, such as a display name, account information, support messages, survey responses, or other information you choose to submit.

Our games and services may also automatically receive technical and gameplay information, such as device type, operating system, game version, language, approximate region, crash reports, performance data, gameplay events, achievements, settings, and identifiers supplied by a device, game platform, or service provider.

2. How We Use Information

We may use information to provide and operate our games; save settings and progress; enable game features; troubleshoot crashes and technical problems; improve performance, balance, usability, and security; respond to support requests; prevent fraud, cheating, abuse, or misuse; comply with legal obligations; and communicate important information about our Services.

3. Platform Services and Third Parties

Our games may be distributed through or interact with third-party platforms and services, such as console manufacturers, PC storefronts, mobile app stores, cloud services, analytics providers, crash-reporting services, advertising providers, or multiplayer/network providers. Those third parties may process information under their own privacy policies. We encourage you to review the privacy settings and policies of the platform or service you use.

4. Advertising and Analytics

Some of our titles may use analytics to understand game performance and how features are used. If a game includes advertising, advertising partners may process device or advertising identifiers and related information as permitted by applicable law and your device or platform privacy settings. Where required, we will request consent or provide appropriate choices before using information for personalized advertising.

5. Purchases

Purchases may be processed by the storefront or platform through which you obtained a game. We generally does not receive your complete payment-card information from those platforms. We may receive transaction-related information needed to confirm purchases, provide content, handle refunds or support, and maintain business records.

6. Children

Our games may have different age ratings and intended audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in a manner that violates applicable law. If a Service is directed to children or we learn that a user is a child, we will apply any additional protections required by applicable law. A parent or guardian who believes a child has provided personal information improperly may contact us to request review or deletion.

7. Data Sharing

We may share information with service providers that help us operate, secure, analyze, distribute, or support our Services; with game platforms when necessary for platform functionality; when required by law or valid legal process; to protect the rights, safety, and security of users, B. Phansaithong Holdings, or others; or as part of a merger, acquisition, financing, sale, or transfer of all or part of our business. We do not sell personal information for money.

8. Data Retention and Security

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including providing the Services, maintaining appropriate business records, resolving disputes, enforcing agreements, and meeting legal obligations. We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards, but no system or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including rights to request access, correction, deletion, portability, or restriction of certain processing, or to object to certain uses. You may also have the right to withdraw consent where processing is based on consent. We may need to verify a request before fulfilling it, and some exceptions may apply under applicable law.

10. International Processing

Our Services and service providers may operate in multiple countries. Information may therefore be processed outside the country where you live. Where required, we use appropriate measures for international transfers of personal information.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as our games, services, or legal obligations change. When we make changes, we will update the effective date above and may provide additional notice when appropriate.
